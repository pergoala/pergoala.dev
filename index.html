Light structure in the right place.

Pergoala (pergola + goal) is an open development methodology, currently in the making, for teams building software together with AI.

A pergola is a light supporting frame: a few posts and beams that give a fast-growing vine hold in the wind, shelter overhead, and direction upward. Neither a wall nor a cage — it spans domains and holds them together.

Strict where change is expensive. Loose where it is cheap.

PERGOLA + GOAL GROW GOAL BY GOAL
The Core Idea

People have background knowledge, but little attention.
The AI has time, but no background knowledge.

Pergoala builds the two paths between them — so teams stay aligned at AI speed without drowning in unread prose or letting the AI guess the product.

PEOPLE → AI

Ordered background knowledge

Short, structured, machine-checked product and architecture context — so AI agents work from real decisions instead of inventing them.

AI → PEOPLE

Reviewable views & an advising agent

Small batches, diagrams, and tables a person can review quickly — paired with an agent that advises, spots connections, and proposes ideas beside every role.

Four Invariant Questions

Roles differ by company. Four questions stay clear.

01 · ASKED

What was asked

Gap-finding before code, with examples and tables both humans and machines can verify.

02 · AGREED

What was agreed

A durable backbone only where change is costly; flexible everywhere else.

03 · TRUE NOW

What is true now

Built, accepted, and shipped stay distinct. Every claim has a guardian or is regenerated.

04 · WHO ACTS

Who acts next

One owner per waiting item and clean handovers across humans and agents.

STATUS · COMING SOON
